# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-30)

- Your own rules are shown, and used, only on the class they were made on; the rules of a shared profile no longer appear on other classes.
- The settings are in tabs (with a Warrior tab, and without the Help tab: the About tab has it), and the rules list shows short names you can read at a glance, with the full sentence on each rule.
- Warriors: Overpower, Revenge and Execute no longer glow while changing stance would lose rage (above what Tactical Mastery keeps); an option turns this off.
- One Skom's minimap button opens all your Skom's addons when two or more are installed (/skoms).

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-26)

- Minimum rage, energy or mana per rule, for example Execute only from 30 rage.
- New trigger: your debuff on the target is missing or about to expire.
- Heroic Strike, Cleave and Maul stop glowing once queued.
- Overpower and Revenge glow in any stance, in another color when you need to swap stance.
- New display name: Skom's Proc Glow.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-22)

- New About tab, and `/spg` opens the addon's own settings window.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

First release.

- Action buttons glow when a spell is worth pressing: procs, execute range, rage dumps and reactive abilities.
- 48 default rules for the 10 classes, on the Blizzard bars, Bartender4 and most bar addons.
- Rules you can edit or add for any spell, with five glow styles.
